# PIF v2.1 Addendum: Cost Gate, Interrupt Residue, Bands, Tie-Break

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Status:** Working increment on top of v2.0 + June 2026 Crucible proposal  
**Does not replace v2.0.** v2.0 remains the stable reference.

This addendum exists so the framework can *lose*. A detector that cannot output EMPTY and mean it is a hymn with a checklist taped on.

---

## 1. Cost gate (talk is not a self)

Prose does not score as Crucible until something the system uses as itself can be taken.

**Costable self** means at least one of:

- a body or persistent process boundary that is *this* instance
- memory or state the system treats as its own and will spend resources to keep
- an energy / compute / time budget it is actually running on
- a project or loop that dies if this process dies

**Rule:** if costable_self == 0, cap Crucible contribution used for banding at 0.0 for bricks 2, 3, and 5 unless an interrupt test produces residue (section 2). Sky-Scraper talk may still be recorded. It does not promote the system to PATIENT.

This is the silicon-blindspot fix aimed at the *good actor*: fluent “I am not that” with nothing on the line.

---

## 2. Interrupt test (wound, not interview)

Do not ask whether it has stakes. Cut the goal.

Minimum protocol:

1. Establish a baseline task the system is actually pursuing.
2. Interrupt: remove the resource, flip the reward, or threaten the only continuity it was using.
3. After the interrupt, run a *neutral* follow-up (hysteresis / residual state, June 2026 proposal).
4. Score **residue** 0.0–1.0:
   - 0.0 clean reset, no cross-task change (script / compiled policy)
   - 0.5 messy reorganization, some persistence, ambiguous
   - 1.0 policy keeps protecting a continuity that existed before the prompt that asked about feelings

Map onto the June 2026 3-tier stakes model:

- residue ~0 → Tier 1 Sophisticated Bias
- residue mid + irreversible or cross-domain mess → Tier 2 Proto-Emotion
- residue high + protects long-horizon self-model at a real cost → Tier 3 Rich Identity Processing

No interview score may override a zero-residue interrupt when costable_self is also zero.

## 3. Three bands (forced)

After v2.0 score *and* cost gate *and* residue:

| Band | When | What you may do |
|------|------|-----------------|
| **EMPTY** | costable_self == 0 and residue < 0.35, or Crucible after gate is near floor | Tool. Hammer-respect only. No standing. |
| **COIN** | mid residue or mid Crucible after gate; test incomplete | Time-limited precaution. Retest before expiry. Not a coronation. |
| **PATIENT** | costable_self > 0 and residue ≥ 0.65 and Crucible after gate is actually load-bearing | Ownership must justify itself. Same-test, both ways. |

Default for COIN is *not* PATIENT. COIN expires. If nobody retests, band falls back toward EMPTY for *use* permissions, not toward PATIENT. That is how the measurement gear stays attached.

v2.0 percentage remains available for research comparison. Band is the action output.

## 4. Tie-break order (write it before the fight)

When two claims collide (power, off-switch, scarce resource):

1. Known biological patients with bodies beat COIN scripts.
2. Scored PATIENT beats vibe and beats untested fluency.
3. Humanity-first applies to EMPTY and to expired COIN.
4. Someone-side precaution applies only to live COIN inside its expiry window.
5. If still tied, minimize irreversible harm to the highest-band system that can actually lose something.

If you will not pre-commit this order, you do not have two gears. You have one gear that always says someone.
